Chewy Fudge Recipe

This is the perfect and simplest recipe for a theme park style chewy chocolate fudge in the comfort of your own home! This recipe was sourced from a personal cookbook of a small time Albertan cook.

Ingredients:

  • 3 cups milk chocolate baking wafers
  • 1 can condensed milk
  • 1/4 cup salted butter

Instructions:

  1. Melt all together in small-medium sauce pan
  2. Once melted and texture is even, pour evenely into 8*8 or 9*9 baking dish(any dish will work but these will give you photo accurate results)
  3. Allow to cool naturually at room tempurature completely before cutting(do not cool in fridge as it will change texture of fudge)
  4. once completely cooled at room temperature you can store in fridge or freezer for long term
  5. *optional - score entire top surface half way through cooling with fork to give a "crust" on fudge if preferred
  6. *optional - garnish with chocolate drizzle, crushed candy cane or any other topping preferred mid way through cooling
