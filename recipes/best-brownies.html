Best Brownies

This is the best frosted brownie recipe I have ever tried, sourced from a personal cookbook from a small time Albertan cook.

Follow the recipe below to try for yourself!

Batter ingredients:

  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 3/4 cup flour
  • 1/8 tsp salt
  • 1/4 cup cocoa
  • 1/2 cup salted butter
  • 2 large eggs

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 350°
  2. Melt butter and cocoa in small pot over low heat(~2)
  3. Whisk sugar, flour and salt together in medium mixing bowl
  4. Beat eggs until frothy, add to dry mix *do not mix yet*
  5. Add cocoa/butter mixture and mix all together, do not overmix or brownies will be tough!
  6. Grease sides and bottom of 8*8 or 9*9 baking dish(pyrex/glass gives best results) add batter and bake at 350° for 30-38 mins until edges start to lift away from pan
  7. Allow to cool completely before frosting(do not cool in fridge/freezer as it will change texture of brownies)

Frosting ingredients:

  • 1 1/3 cups icing sugar
  • 1/3 cup cocoa
  • 1/4 cup salted butter
  • 2 TBSP milk, hot water or coffee

Instructions:

  1. Fold/cream 1 cup icing sugar into butter, do not use mixer at this point or the icing will break, turn lumpy/grainy or will look curdled. This stop must be done by hand to properly constitute the icing.
  2. Repeat with remaining icing sugar
  3. Use electric mixer to mix in 1 Tbsp liquid with cocoa
  4. Add in 2nd Tbsp liquid slowly while mixing to get desired texture(icing should remain quite thick)
