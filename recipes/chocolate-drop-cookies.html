Chocolate drop oatmeal cookies

Delicious no bake chocolate drop oatmeal cookies ready in no time!

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups white sugar
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/4 cup cocoa
  • 3 cups oats
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Instructions:

  1. In medium-large saucepan bring sugar, butter and milk to rolling boil while stirring constantly for 1 minute
  2. Mix in cocoa, oats and vanilla
  3. Spoon drop onto wax paper and allow to cool
